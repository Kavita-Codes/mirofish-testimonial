
# Instagram Reel Campaign – Test 01

## Objective
Evaluate 10 unpublished Instagram Reels and identify which content has the strongest potential for organic engagement.

## Target Audience
- Indian Gen Z
- College students
- Young professionals
- Instagram entertainment viewers
- Educational content consumers
- Content creators

## Reel 1
Topic: Relatable college life
Hook: Every college student has experienced this!
Caption: Tag your college friend.
Hashtags: #CollegeLife #StudentLife #Relatable

## Reel 2
Topic: AI tools for students
Hook: These AI tools can save you hours!
Caption: Save this list for later.
Hashtags: #AITools #StudentTips #Technology

## Reel 3
Topic: Emotional storytelling
Hook: Sometimes the smallest moments matter most.
Caption: Share this with someone special.
Hashtags: #Storytelling #Emotions #Life

## Simulation Requirements
Create diverse audience personas.
Simulate how they might react to these content ideas.
Evaluate hook strength, relevance, originality, shareability and possible reasons for skipping.
Compare the Reels and recommend improvements.

Do not claim actual views or guaranteed virality.
